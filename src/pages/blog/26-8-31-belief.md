I've noticed that in the past, I would sometimes hesitate to send an email to someone.

Maybe because I've been given the cold shoulder many a time in the past.

Or maybe because I had the shittiest day ever.

Or perhaps by lack of sleep, I'd have the fear that I would not be able to draft some mail with prose pretty enough to get what I wanted.

But, maybe that applies to others as well.

Maybe the other person got the worst kind of sleep last time.

Or that guy that didn't respond had these thoughts as well.

Maybe they were sick, or didn't have the time.

---

Reading about how Marilu Henner's Hypersynesthesia developed made me realize many things.

That perhaps the mechanisms that underly our actions are not at all universal.

And, if that's true, all that's left to ask is, is there any room at all for any change in that mechanism, at any age?

And if the answer to that is yes, then how can we hold others in high contempt?

If you believe that you can change, then others can too.

Then the you of yesterday is not the you of today, and neither are they.

Then the chain of contempt, which really binds yourself, is unbroken.

And the world becomes a bit foggier, dissipating in the light...but also a bit more casual, and mischievous.
